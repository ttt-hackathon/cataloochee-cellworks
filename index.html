Battery packs for electric bikes, built in the Smoky Mountains.

Since 2014, Cataloochee Cell Works has designed and assembled lithium-ion battery packs for North American e-bike makers.

Customers: production and shipping updates are posted on our Customer advisories page as soon as we know them.

Products

CCW-625 frame-integrated pack

625 Wh, 36 V. Built for mid-drive and hub-drive systems, with a sealed aluminum housing and a 1,000-cycle rating.

CCW-500 rack-mount pack

500 Wh, 36 V. A removable rear-rack pack for commuter and cargo bikes.

Custom packs

Custom shapes and capacities for OEM customers with orders of 2,000 units a year or more.

Our plant

All packs are assembled, tested and shipped from our plant in Waynesville, North Carolina, with two cell assembly lines and a full testing lab.

We build packs from lithium-ion cells made by Samsung SDI in South Korea, which arrive through the Port of Savannah.
